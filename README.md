Carmesine — Exploratory Tests on Crimson Roman for Legal and Academic Workflows

This repository presents a set of exploratory tests performed on Crimson Roman (2012) to evaluate its behaviour in legal and academic writing environments. The goal is to understand how the typeface performs under digital‑first workflows, PDF rendering inconsistencies, and low‑quality office printing, which increasingly affect long‑form professional documents.

These tests were carried out in FontForge, supported by AI‑assisted analysis (Microsoft Copilot), focusing on practical aspects such as:
- paragraph colour
- x‑height behaviour
- Roman/Italic balance
- OTF vs TTF rendering robustness
- stability under weak anti‑aliasing and coarse rasterization
- The findings are practical observations, not typographic doctrine, and aim to highlight how Crimson can be adapted or reconsidered for contemporary document‑production realities.
